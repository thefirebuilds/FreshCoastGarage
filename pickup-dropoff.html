ABIA Airport Pickup

The Parking Spot – West
1600 E Highway 71, Austin, TX 78742

Land→Shuttle→Unlock & go

01 Hop on the shuttle

At the terminal’s shuttle pickup area, look for the yellow bus with black dots. Confirm it’s heading to The Parking Spot – West.

02 Your car is waiting

Use your reservation instructions to open the car’s lockbox. Follow the blue exit signs; gates should open automatically.

03 Park, lock & fly

Return to the same lot and lock the keys inside the car. We’ll use our spare. Take the shuttle back to the terminal.

Driving in? Highway 71 only.Direct airport access is restricted to shuttles.
