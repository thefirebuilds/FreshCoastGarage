Booking & changes

How can I check availability for my dates?

Start with Our Cars, then open the vehicle on Turo and enter your exact pickup and return dates and times. Turo shows availability and pricing for your selected trip; the website cards are a quick overview.

Can I extend my trip?

Request an extension through your Turo reservation before your scheduled return. Extensions depend on availability and confirmation in Turo, and the price may change. Until the extension is confirmed, your original return time still applies.

Can I change my pickup or check-in time?

Message us through your reservation as soon as your plans change. Request any needed trip-time change in Turo and wait for confirmation before arriving earlier. A later pickup does not automatically extend your return time.

Are there extra delivery or pickup fees?

Fees depend on the pickup location and reservation. Review the price breakdown in Turo before booking or confirming a location change. Guests do not pay the parking fee for our The Parking Spot – West arrangement; other trip or delivery charges may still apply.

What is the cancellation policy? Is cancellation within an hour fully refundable?

Refund eligibility depends on your booking type and timing, so a full refund within an hour is not a blanket guarantee. Check the cancellation terms in your Turo reservation before canceling. See Turo’s cancellation guidance for the current rules.

Finding your car & checking in

Where do I pick up my car?

Vehicles are generally available from our Buda location near FM 967 & FM 1626 or The Parking Spot – West near Austin-Bergstrom International Airport. Your reservation identifies your pickup location. See our pickup and dropoff guide for the steps.

When and how do I send my license photos?

Follow the identity and license verification prompts in your Turo reservation before pickup. Upload the requested photos through Turo’s verification flow when prompted, and bring your valid driver’s license. Message us through the reservation if you have trouble completing check-in.

How do I find the car when I arrive?

Use the exact pickup location and vehicle instructions in your reservation. For airport pickup, take The Parking Spot – West shuttle to the lot first. If you cannot find the car, message us through your reservation so we can help.

How does airport pickup work?

Your car will be waiting at The Parking Spot – West near ABIA. Take The Parking Spot – West shuttle from the airport terminal to the lot, then use the lockbox on the vehicle for contactless pickup. Follow the blue exit signs when leaving; the gates should open automatically.

Where is The Parking Spot – West?

The lot is at 1600 E Highway 71, Austin, TX 78742. When driving to the lot, you must enter from Highway 71. The direct access from the airport is restricted to shuttles and cannot be used by guests.

How do I recognize the airport shuttle?

Look for The Parking Spot’s yellow shuttle bus with black polka dots. Confirm it’s the shuttle to the West lot before boarding.

What if I have trouble finding the shuttle?

Call The Parking Spot – West at (512) 385-9343 for shuttle help.

How does Buda pickup work?

Your vehicle will be prepared before your trip at our Buda pickup location near FM 967 & FM 1626. Pickup is contactless using a lockbox. Exact pickup instructions are provided with your reservation. Return to the same location unless otherwise arranged, and lock the keys inside the car. We’ll use our spare key to collect it.

Why isn’t the exact Buda address listed on the website?

The Buda pickup point is a private location, so the exact address and pickup instructions are provided directly to booked guests.

Is pickup contactless?

Yes. Vehicles are generally prepared in advance and secured with a lockbox so you can begin your trip without coordinating an in-person key exchange.

What if I have a problem during pickup?

Message Fresh Coast Garage through your reservation. For airport shuttle-specific issues, call The Parking Spot – West at (512) 385-9343.

During your trip

Are Texas toll roads supported?

Yes. Our vehicles have a Texas-compatible toll tag. You can use toll roads normally, and toll charges are billed after the trip according to your reservation terms.

What if the car has a mechanical issue?

If the car seems unsafe to drive, stop somewhere safe and do not continue driving. Message us through your reservation and contact Turo roadside assistance for a breakdown or roadside issue. In an emergency, call 911.

Can I keep the complimentary items in the car?

The cords and lotion are yours to keep — enjoy! Please leave the sunshade and 12V USB charger in the car when you return it.

Returns & the little details

Can I return the car to a different location?

Please ask us through your reservation before changing the return location. A different dropoff point depends on availability, confirmation, and any applicable fees. Until a change is confirmed in your reservation, return to the original location.

Where do I return an airport vehicle?

Return the vehicle to The Parking Spot – West, entering from Highway 71. The direct airport access is for shuttles only. Park the car and lock the keys inside; we’ll use our spare key to collect it. Then take The Parking Spot shuttle back to the airport terminal.

Where should I leave the keys on return?

Lock the keys inside the car when you return it to the agreed location. We’ll use our spare key to collect the vehicle. Check for your phone, wallet, luggage, and other belongings before locking up.

Should I clean the car before returning it?

We take care of the cleaning, so please don’t wash the outside. Just keep the inside tidy and take your rubbish and personal belongings with you when you return the car.

Do I have to pay for airport parking?

No. Fresh Coast Garage guests do not pay the parking fee when picking up or returning one of our vehicles through this arrangement.
